Personalized customer experiences depend on knowing who a customer is and what they are doing now, across every channel where you meet them. Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform (Real-Time CDP) addresses this by bringing customer data together into profiles that update as new data arrives, and making those profiles available to the channels that engage customers. This article covers the key features and benefits of Real-Time CDP and how it can improve customer engagement.

## What is Adobe Real-Time CDP?

Adobe Real-Time CDP is a cloud-based application built on [Adobe Experience Platform](/adobe-services/experience-platform). It collects customer data from many sources, unifies it into customer profiles, builds audiences from those profiles, and activates them to marketing, advertising and personalization channels. It comes in B2C, B2B and B2P editions, for consumer data, account and lead data, or both together.

## Key features of Adobe Real-Time CDP

### Unified customer profiles

Real-Time CDP brings together data from sources such as websites, mobile apps, CRM systems, loyalty programs, point-of-sale systems and data warehouses. Identity Service links the identifiers that belong to the same person, and Real-Time Customer Profile merges the data into one profile that covers both known and anonymous behavior. This gives a fuller picture of each customer's preferences, behavior and needs.

### Real-time data processing

Streaming data updates profiles as customers interact, so audiences and personalization can reflect their most recent actions. Not every audience needs to work this way: Real-Time CDP evaluates audiences in batch (every 24 hours), as data streams in, or at the edge for same-page and next-page personalization on websites. Matching the method to the use case keeps the setup simpler.

### Audiences and segmentation

Marketers build audiences from attributes, behaviors and events, such as demographics, purchase history, product interest or recent site activity, using a visual audience builder. Real-Time CDP can also bring in audiences created elsewhere. Well-defined audiences keep marketing focused on the people most likely to respond.

### Activation and integration

A catalog of destinations sends audiences and profile data to advertising platforms, email and marketing automation tools, CRM, cloud storage and personalization tools. That includes other Adobe Experience Cloud applications, such as Adobe Target for web personalization and Adobe Journey Optimizer and Adobe Campaign for messaging. Data collected for one channel can then improve engagement in all of them.

### Privacy and compliance

Real-Time CDP includes data governance controls that help businesses use customer data in line with regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA. Data usage labels and policies are checked when an audience is activated, and an activation that would break a policy is blocked. Consent policies, available with the Adobe Healthcare Shield or Adobe Privacy & Security Shield add-ons, exclude profiles that lack the required consent. Requests from individuals to access or delete their data can be handled through Adobe Experience Platform Privacy Service.

## Benefits of Adobe Real-Time CDP

### Personalization based on recent actions

A unified, current view of each customer lets businesses tailor experiences across touchpoints to what a customer has just done, not only to their history. Relevant experiences support satisfaction and loyalty.

### Visibility into how audiences change

Profile and audience dashboards show how audiences are built and how they change over time. Paired with an analytics application such as Customer Journey Analytics, the same unified data helps teams understand behavior and refine their marketing strategy.

### Fewer manual list exports

Real-Time CDP replaces manual list exports, one-off integrations and spreadsheet matching with governed, repeatable data flows. Teams spend less time reconciling data silos and more time on the experiences themselves.

### Less wasted spend

Precise audiences, activated quickly to the right channels, help businesses reach the right customers with the right message at the right time. Suppressing audiences who have already converted also reduces wasted spend.
