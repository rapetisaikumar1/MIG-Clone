Customer experience (CX) is one of the clearest ways a business can stand out, and good experiences depend on good customer data. Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) provides the data foundation that Adobe's customer experience applications are built on. This article covers the key capabilities of Experience Platform and how they support a customer experience strategy.

## What is Adobe Experience Platform?

Adobe Experience Platform collects customer data from many sources, standardizes it, and makes it available for analysis, personalization and activation. It is the foundation for applications such as [Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform](/adobe-services/real-time-cdp), Adobe Journey Optimizer and Customer Journey Analytics, and most organizations use it through those applications.

At its core are a few shared services: Experience Data Model (XDM) schemas that give data from different systems a common structure, Identity Service to link the identifiers that belong to the same person, and Real-Time Customer Profile to bring that data together.

## Key capabilities of Adobe Experience Platform

### Unified customer profiles

Real-Time Customer Profile merges data from multiple sources, such as web and app behavior, CRM, loyalty and point-of-sale systems, into one profile per person. Profiles cover both known and anonymous identifiers, giving a fuller view of each customer's interactions across channels. How well profiles merge depends on identity design, so it deserves attention early in any implementation.

### Streaming and batch data ingestion

Experience Platform accepts data in batches and as streams. Streaming ingestion, including data collected through the Adobe Experience Platform Web SDK and Edge Network, keeps profiles updated as customers act, so experiences can respond to what a customer just did. Batch ingestion suits data that changes less often, such as daily exports from other systems.

### Audiences and segmentation

Segmentation Service builds audiences from profile data based on attributes and behaviors. Audiences can be evaluated in batch (automatically every 24 hours), by streaming segmentation as new data arrives, or by edge segmentation for same-page and next-page personalization. Choosing the right method for each use case matters: not every audience needs to update in real time.

### AI and machine learning

Experience Platform includes AI and machine learning capabilities. Intelligent Services such as Customer AI and Attribution AI predict customer behavior, such as the likelihood to convert or churn, and measure how marketing touchpoints contribute to outcomes. Adobe has also added generative AI features, such as AI Assistant, to help teams explore data and work with the platform. Availability depends on the applications you license, and predictions are only as good as the data behind them.

### Integration with Adobe Experience Cloud and other systems

Experience Platform connects to other Adobe Experience Cloud applications, such as Adobe Analytics, Adobe Target and Adobe Campaign, and to non-Adobe systems through a catalog of source connectors and destinations. This lets data collected in one place support analytics, personalization and campaigns elsewhere.

### Data governance

Data usage labels and policies control how data may be used, and Experience Platform checks them when data is activated. This makes it practical to respect consent and contractual restrictions as data moves between systems.

## Benefits of Adobe Experience Platform

### More relevant personalization

With unified profiles that update as customers interact, businesses can tailor experiences to a customer's current context, not just their history, which supports loyalty and satisfaction.

### Better customer insights

A consistent, connected view of customer data makes it easier to understand behavior and preferences across channels, and to refine strategy based on what customers actually do.

### Greater efficiency

Centralizing data and automating its flow between systems reduces the manual work of exporting, matching and loading customer data, so teams can spend more time on the experiences themselves.

### Better marketing ROI

Precise audiences, activated to the right channels at the right time, help campaigns reach the people most likely to respond and reduce wasted spend.
